Methodology & Sources — how these rankings were built ▶

What this is

The tier lists are a Season 2 projection — a forward estimate from announced changes, not live performance data (Season 2 isn't out yet). PTR numbers are not final and will shift before launch.

Sources

  • End-of-Season-1 baseline: Icy Veins Mythic+ tier lists (Patch 12.0.7, July 2026, by Petko): DPS · Tank · Healer
  • Season 2 class changes (Patch 12.1 PTR): Icy Veins — PTR class tuning · NextTier — full per-spec breakdown
  • Dungeon pool & tuning: Blizzard dungeon picks & design (Icy Veins) · July 21 dungeon tuning notes

Method — three layers

  • 1. Season 1 baseline. Every spec starts from its end-of-Season-1 tier (toolkit, damage profile, survivability, utility).
  • 2. Three combat-wide system shifts in 12.1:
    • +25% player health & enemy damage → rewards sustain/durability, penalizes glass-cannon burst and twitch-healing.
    • Cooldown smoothing (power out of burst windows into filler) → steady-damage specs gain relative value.
    • DPS role-share emphasis (bigger enemy HP, priority targeting, damage checks) → strong priority/funnel damage gets a bump.
  • 3. Per-spec PTR changes. Each spec's specific 12.1 tuning/rework moves it up, down, or holds it based on whether the changes are a net buff, nerf, or rework toward consistency.

Example calls

  • Protection Paladin A→A+: largest tank rework (+100% Judgment/Consecration, +150% SotR, +30% Avenger's Shield, Blessed Word, +20% HP Ardent Defender) fixes S1's squishiness.
  • Restoration Druid B→A+: widest healer rework (Overgrowth, Flash of Clarity, redesigned Innervate, Tree of Life, broad healing buffs + mana cuts).
  • Marksmanship Hunter C→A: biggest single-spec overhaul (Explosive Shot rework, core +20%, auto-shot +600%, Hunter's Mark on 5 targets).
  • Elemental Shaman →A+: Ascendance de-emphasized but core spells +30–60% — far more consistent, which the smoothing era favors.
  • Blood Death Knight C→B: big survivability sweep synergizes with the +25% health system.

Limitations

  • Projection, not measurement. Reasoning forward from announced changes; revisit once live log data exists.
  • PTR is a first build. Blizzard has said they will watch and adjust before launch.
  • Tier boundaries involve judgment. A+ vs A (and A vs B) are calls; a few specs could sit one tier differently and be defensible.
  • Scope: average/intermediate key-level meta, not PvP, raid, or high-key-only outliers.

As always — bring the better player, not just the better spec.
