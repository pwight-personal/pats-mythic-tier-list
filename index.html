Methodology & Sources — how these rankings were built ▶

What this is

The tier lists follow the Season 2 Mythic+ PTR meta (Patch 12.1) using community tier lists from Petko / Icy Veins (progressive PTR lists updated weekly, last July 26, 2026), blended with measurable performance data from log sites. These are progressive, subjective lists — not final, and they change weekly as PTR tuning lands.

Sources

  • Community PTR tier lists (primary): Icy Veins / Petko, Patch 12.1 PTR: DPS · Tank · Healer
  • Meta overview: Icy Veins — Season 2 meta is forming
  • Measurable performance (damage/HPS): Warcraft Logs — M+ S2 damage · Archon — PTR M+ DPS · U.GG — healer HPS/DPS
  • Dungeon pool & tuning: Blizzard dungeon picks & design · July 21 dungeon tuning

How specs are measured (by role)

  • DPS — ranked by raw damage across profiles (single-target, 2–3-target, mass AoE, burst AoE, sustained AoE, priority/funnel) plus survivability, self-sustain, and party-wide buffs/ utility. Measured via Warcraft Logs / Archon damage rankings.
  • Healers — ranked by HPS throughput and personal DPS output (Discipline, Mistweaver, Preservation punch well above their weight in damage), plus external cooldowns, utility, mana efficiency, and durability. Measured via U.GG HPS/DPS and Warcraft Logs.
  • Tanks — ranked by a blend of durability/survivability, personal DPS, self-sustain, mobility, and party-wide utility/externals. Damage mitigation and tank DPS both factor in.

Notable community calls (this build)

  • Arcane Mage & Balance Druid rise to S after weak showings — Arcane's Apex/Pulse rework and durability pass; Balance's damage-distribution shift.
  • Devourer Demon Hunter falls to A+ — Mastery & Void Meta nerfs gutted the Collapsing Star build; new builds are inferior so far.
  • Holy Paladin promoted to S (healer) after multiple HPS buff rounds; Mistweaver demoted to A+ after repeated HPS nerfs (DPS still top-tier).
  • Protection Paladin is the new top tank (biggest rework; demoted from S to A+ only after consecutive nerfs); Guardian Druid drops to B+ after its Apex revamp cut its damage.
  • Augmentation Evoker & Vengeance DH = TBD — too early to call final power.

Limitations

  • PTR, not live. Small parse counts (Archon PTR in the hundreds); beta analytics are explicitly not for balance conclusions.
  • Progressive & subjective. Opinion tier lists updated weekly; reasonable players disagree on border tiers.
  • Tier boundaries involve judgment. A+ vs A, B+ vs B are calls; a few specs could sit one tier differently and be defensible.
  • Scope: average/intermediate key-level M+ meta, not PvP, raid, or high-key-only outliers.

As always — bring the better player, not just the better spec.
